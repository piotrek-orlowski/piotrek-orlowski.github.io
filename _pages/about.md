<p><span style="font-size:1.5em"> This is the personal page of Piotr Orłowski. Thank you for stopping by.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:1.3em"> <nobr>I am</nobr> Associate Professor of Finance at <a href="https://www.hec.ca/">HEC Montréal</a> and Scientific Director at the <a href="https://cdi-icd.org/en/">Canadian Derivatives Institute</a>. My <a href="{{base_path | append: "/publications/"}}">research</a> interests center on the econometrics of asset pricing and derivatives. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:1.3em"> <nobr>I teach</nobr> courses in financial econometrics, investment and derivatives at the undergraduate and graduate level.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:1.3em"> Every year, <nobr>I organize</nobr>  two events: the <a href="https://cdi-icd.org/en/annual-conference/">Annual Conference</a> of the CDI, in collaboration with <a href="https://www.hec.ca/en/profs/christian.dorion.html">Christian Dorion</a>, and the HEC-McGill Winter Finance Workshop, in collaboration with <a href="https://www.mcgill.ca/desautels/jan-ericsson">Jan Ericsson</a>.</span></p>
